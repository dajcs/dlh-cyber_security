### KPI Interpretation

- **Average mitigation time:** 38 days is above the 30-day target. The downward trend indicates improvement, but remediation speed still needs attention.
- **Control effectiveness:** 86% is below the 90% target, although the upward trend shows that recent control improvements are having a positive effect.
- **Open action items:** 14 remains above the target of 10. The downward trend indicates that backlog reduction efforts are working.

## Section 4: Quarterly Actions

- **Action 1:** Complete remediation of all Critical-risk findings, including SQL injection vulnerabilities and cloud storage misconfigurations.
- **Action 2:** Reduce average risk mitigation time from 38 days to 30 days or less by prioritizing overdue High and Critical remediation actions.
- **Action 3:** Improve control effectiveness from 86% to at least 90% through control testing, remediation tracking, and validation of security controls.

---

## Board-Level Summary

SecureBank currently has **20 tracked risks**, including **2 Critical** and **5 High** risks. The immediate priority is to close Critical-risk remediation items while improving the speed and effectiveness of the overall risk treatment process.

The quarterly dashboard indicates progress in control effectiveness and action-item reduction, but both remain below target. Management should focus the next quarter on **Critical-risk closure, faster mitigation, and measurable improvement in control performance**.
