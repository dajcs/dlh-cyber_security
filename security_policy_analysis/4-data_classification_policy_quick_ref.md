# Data Classification – Quick Reference Guide

**HealthPlus Medical Group** · Version 1.0 · Effective August 7, 2026

---

## The 4 Levels at a Glance

| Level | What it means | Examples |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| 🟢 **PUBLIC** | OK to share publicly | Marketing, website content |
| 🔵 **INTERNAL** | Staff only | Memos, org charts, directories |
| 🟠 **CONFIDENTIAL** | Could cause harm if leaked | Employee PII, financials, contracts |
| 🔴 **RESTRICTED** | Severe harm if leaked | Patient records (PHI), passwords, keys |

**Not sure? Treat it as CONFIDENTIAL and ask the Security & Compliance Team.**
**Mixing data? Protect it all at the highest level present.**

## What You Must Do

| | 🟢 Public | 🔵 Internal | 🟠 Confidential | 🔴 Restricted |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Label it?** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Encrypt when stored?** | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Encrypt when sent?** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Who can access?** | Anyone | All staff | By role / need | By role + **MFA** |

---

## Quick Rules

**📁 Storing**
- Confidential & Restricted → company-approved, encrypted systems only.
- Never on personal devices, personal cloud (Dropbox/iCloud), or unencrypted USBs.
- PHI → EHR or HIPAA-compliant systems only.

**📧 Sending**
- Confidential/Restricted → use approved **encrypted** email or secure portal.
- Never email PHI or passwords to personal accounts.
- Double-check the recipient before you hit send.

**🗑️ Disposing**
- Paper (Confidential/Restricted) → **shred** or use locked shred bins.
- Devices/USBs → give to IT for secure wipe or destruction. Deleting a file is **not** enough.

**🔑 Access**
- Only access what your job needs (least privilege).
- Never share your login. Use MFA where required.
- Report anything suspicious right away.

Report it **immediately**. Fast reporting protects patients and helps us meet legal deadlines.
