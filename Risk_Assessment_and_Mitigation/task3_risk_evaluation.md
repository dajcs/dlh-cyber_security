# Task 3 - Risk Evaluation


## Scenario
**SecureBank** has a Critical risk with an **Annual Loss Expectancy (ALE) of $300,000**.

### Proposed Mitigation
- Implementation cost: **$80,000**
- Annual maintenance: **$15,000**
- Expected risk reduction: **75%**

## Cost-Benefit Analysis

| **Metric** | **Calculation** | **Value** |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| Current ALE | Given | $300,000 |
| New ALE after mitigation | $300,000 × (1 - 0.75) | $75,000 |
| Annual Savings | $300,000 - $75,000 | $225,000 |
| 3-Year Total Cost | $80,000 + ($15,000 × 3) | $125,000 |
| 3-Year Total Savings | $225,000 × 3 | $675,000 |
| Net Benefit | $675,000 - $125,000 | $550,000 |
| ROI | ($550,000 / $125,000) × 100 | 440% |

## Decision

**Implement mitigation (cost-effective).**

## Justification

The mitigation reduces the annual expected loss from **$300,000 to $75,000**, producing annual risk savings of **$225,000**.

Over three years, the organization would spend **$125,000** on implementation and maintenance while achieving **$675,000** in expected savings. This results in a **net benefit of $550,000** and an **ROI of 440%**.

Because the expected financial benefit significantly exceeds the total mitigation cost, implementing the mitigation is economically justified.
