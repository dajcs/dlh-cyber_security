## 1. Purpose

TechSecure Solutions Inc. provides employees and other users with access to information technology systems – including a cloud-based development environment, corporate email, VPN, and collaboration tools – to support the company's software development business. These systems are valuable company assets that carry legal, security, and reputational risk when misused.

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) establishes clear, enforceable rules for how company technology and information may be used. Its objective is to protect company data, systems, clients, and staff from harm caused by careless or improper use, while allowing employees to work productively.

This policy has been developed in direct response to recent incidents at the company, including the use of work email for personal matters, unauthorized software installations, social media posts disclosing confidential project information, and personal devices connecting to the corporate network without approval. The requirements below are designed to prevent these situations from recurring.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

This policy applies to:

- [x] All employees (full-time, part-time, and temporary)
- [x] Contractors and consultants
- [x] Third-party vendors with access to company systems
- [x] Interns, trainees, and any other individuals granted access to company technology resources

It applies to all staff across the company's three office locations and to all remote workers, regardless of where they are working from.

### 2.2 Systems/Assets Covered

This policy covers all company-owned, company-managed, or company-authorized technology, including but not limited to:

- Cloud-based development environments and source-code repositories
- Corporate email and messaging systems
- The corporate VPN and internal networks (wired and wireless)
- Collaboration and productivity tools (e.g., chat, video conferencing, document sharing, project management)
- Company-issued laptops, desktops, mobile devices, and peripherals
- Personal devices used to access company systems or data (see Section 3.5)
- All company data stored, processed, or transmitted using any of the above

### 2.3 Exclusions

- Personal devices and personal accounts that are never used to access company systems, data, or networks
- Publicly available, non-confidential company information already approved for release (e.g., marketing materials on the company website)

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Internet Usage

Company internet access is provided primarily for business purposes. Users must browse and use online services responsibly and lawfully.

**Requirements:**
- Reasonable, occasional personal internet use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive bandwidth, or violate any part of this policy.
- Users must not access, download, or distribute material that is illegal, offensive, discriminatory, harassing, or sexually explicit.
- Users must not attempt to bypass web filters, security controls, or monitoring tools.
- Users must not use company internet access for personal financial gain, gambling, or operating a personal business.
- The company monitors and logs internet activity on its systems and networks. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company internet resources.

### 3.2 Email Usage

Corporate email is a business communication tool and an official company record. It must be used professionally.

**Requirements:**
- Corporate email must be used for business communication only. Personal email accounts must be used for personal correspondence, and work email must not be used to sign up for personal services, shopping, or social accounts.
- Users must not send confidential company or client information to personal email addresses or unauthorized recipients.
- Users must not send spam, chain letters, or mass unsolicited messages, and must not forge or misrepresent the sender of a message.
- Users must exercise caution with attachments and links to avoid phishing and malware.
- Email is retained in accordance with the company's records retention schedule (a minimum of three years for business correspondence, longer where legal or contractual obligations apply). Users must not delete emails subject to a legal hold.
- The company may access, monitor, and archive email for security, compliance, and legal purposes.

### 3.3 Software

Only authorized software may be used on company systems to protect against security vulnerabilities, licensing violations, and malware.

**Requirements:**
- Users must not install, download, or run software on company devices or in the development environment without approval from the IT department.
- Only software from the company's approved software list or catalog may be installed. Requests for new software must be submitted to IT through the standard request process and include a business justification.
- Users must not use unlicensed, pirated, or "cracked" software, or bring personal software licenses onto company systems.
- Users must not disable, alter, or remove security software (e.g., antivirus, endpoint protection, patching agents).
- Open-source and third-party libraries used in development must comply with the company's software approval and license-review process.

### 3.4 Social Media

Public statements and posts can affect the company's reputation and disclose confidential information. Users must exercise care on all social and public platforms.

**Requirements:**
- Users must not disclose confidential, proprietary, or client information – including details of internal projects, products, code, clients, or business plans – on any social media or public platform.
- Users must not represent themselves as speaking on behalf of TechSecure Solutions Inc. unless specifically authorized to do so. Personal opinions must be clearly identified as personal.
- Only authorized personnel (e.g., Marketing, Communications) may post on official company accounts.
- Users must not post content that is defamatory, harassing, or discriminatory toward colleagues, clients, or partners.
- When in doubt about whether information is confidential, users must consult their manager before posting.

### 3.5 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices may be used to access company systems only when registered and secured, to protect the corporate network and company data.

**Requirements:**
- Personal devices must be registered with and approved by IT before connecting to the corporate network or accessing company data. Unregistered personal devices must not connect to the corporate network.
- Approved devices must meet minimum security requirements, including a supported operating system, current security updates, a strong passcode or biometric lock, device encryption, and company-approved endpoint or mobile-device-management (MDM) software where required.
- The company reserves the right to enforce security policies on registered devices and to remotely wipe company data from a device that is lost, stolen, or belongs to a departing user.
- Users must report lost or stolen devices that contain or can access company data to IT immediately.
- Company data on personal devices remains company property and is subject to this policy.

### 3.6 Data Handling

Company and client data must be stored, shared, and disposed of securely according to its sensitivity.

**Requirements:**
- Data must be classified and handled according to the company's data classification standard (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted).
- Company and client data must be stored only in approved, company-managed systems and repositories. It must not be stored on unauthorized personal cloud accounts, USB drives, or personal devices.
- Sharing of confidential or restricted data must be limited to authorized recipients on a need-to-know basis, using approved and, where appropriate, encrypted channels.
- Confidential data must be encrypted in transit and at rest wherever technically feasible.
- Data must be disposed of securely when no longer needed and in line with the retention schedule – electronic data through secure deletion and physical media through approved destruction methods.
- Users must not attempt to access data or systems they are not authorized to use.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Executive Management** | Approve the policy, allocate resources, and demonstrate visible commitment to acceptable use. |
| **CISO / IT Security Team** | Own and maintain the policy, implement and monitor technical controls, investigate incidents, and report violations. |
| **IT Department** | Maintain the approved software list, register and secure BYOD devices, provision access, and support users. |
| **Department Managers** | Ensure their teams understand and comply with the policy, report issues, and support training. |
| **All Employees and Users** | Read, understand, and comply with this policy; use company systems responsibly; complete required training; and report incidents and suspected violations promptly. |
| **Human Resources** | Support enforcement, manage disciplinary processes, and ensure the policy is included in onboarding. |

---

## 5. Compliance

### 5.1 Monitoring

The company monitors the use of its systems, networks, email, internet access, and connected devices to ensure compliance, protect security, and detect misuse. Monitoring is conducted lawfully and proportionately, and users should have no expectation of privacy when using company resources.

### 5.2 Reporting

Compliance metrics and significant incidents are reported by the IT Security Team to executive management on a regular basis (at minimum quarterly, and immediately for serious incidents). Users are required to report suspected policy violations, security incidents, and lost or stolen devices without delay.

### 5.3 Auditing

The IT Security Team conducts periodic audits of system access, installed software, BYOD registrations, and data handling practices. Audits are performed at least annually and may occur more frequently in response to incidents or risk assessments. Users must cooperate fully with audits.

---

## 6. Enforcement

### 6.1 Violations

Violations of this policy may result in one or more of the following, depending on severity and circumstances:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension of access privileges
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract
- Legal action where applicable

Contractors, consultants, and vendors who violate this policy may have their access revoked and their contracts terminated.

### 6.2 Reporting Violations

Report suspected violations to the IT Security Team at **security@techsecure.example** or through the internal IT service desk. Reports may be made confidentially, and good-faith reporting will not result in retaliation.

---

## 7. Exceptions

### 7.1 Exception Process

Exceptions to this policy require:

1. A written request submitted to the CISO or the Information Security Committee
2. A clear business justification
3. A risk assessment
4. Compensating controls (if applicable)
5. Formal approval and documentation

### 7.2 Exception Duration

All exceptions must have a defined end date and be reviewed at least annually. Expired exceptions are void unless formally renewed.
